Who it's for

One system for the whole clinic

Everyone gets their own dashboard and only sees what they need.

Patients

  • Register and log in
  • Book or cancel appointments
  • See your queue number and status
  • Update your own profile

Doctors & nurses

  • Confirm appointment requests
  • Add walk-ins, call the next patient
  • Write consultation records
  • View daily reports

Administrators

  • Manage staff accounts
  • Set clinic hours and slot length
  • Manage all patient records
  • Review activity logs
How it works

Three simple steps

Step 1

Create your account

Fill in your details once. It only takes a minute.

Step 2

Book a time slot

Choose a date and an open time, and tell us the reason for your visit.

Step 3

Follow your queue

Check your number on your phone and arrive when it's almost your turn.
